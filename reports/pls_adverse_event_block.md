# PLS adverse-event purchase block: test results

_Generated by `scripts/adverse_event_block.py` on 2026-09-28. Do not hand-edit; rerun the script._

## The rule tested

After a PLS-specific adverse event (production cut, cost increase, outage, funding problem), block new purchases for **5 trading sessions**, counting the first session whose close is after the first report.

## Data

- Events: Bigdata.com search (entity 9561FC, fast and smart modes), PLS Group Ltd / Pilbara Minerals. News window 2020-12-01 to 2026-09-23. 314 stories, deduplicated to 64 events.
- Prices: IBKR daily ASX bars 30 Sep 2021 to 28 Sep 2026, total return (3 dividends added back). Market benchmark: S&P/ASX 200 (XJO).
- Events tested against prices: 61. Events before 30 Sep 2021 are catalogued but untested (no daily price source reachable).
- Candidate buy sessions: 1209; blocked by the rule: 148 (12%).

## Pre-registered pass rules

1. Every-session test: blocked sessions' mean 20-session forward return, market-adjusted, is below allowed sessions' and the placebo p-value is <= 0.10.
2. Post-event drift from the reaction-session close to the close five sessions later is negative on average AND in the median.
3. The block (blocked signals skipped, not deferred) improves at least 2 of the 3 rule-based strategies: dip-buy mean trade return, trend mean trade return, weekly DCA average cost (DCA defers the blocked buy to the first allowed session).

## Verdict: **NOT SUPPORTED**

| 2. Post-event 5-session drift negative | fail |

## Caveats

- One stock, about five years, a few dozen events: low statistical power. Treat p-values as indicative.
- Forward windows overlap, so session-level observations are autocorrelated; the placebo keeps the same series and block structure to account for that.
- The event list depends on what Bigdata.com indexed and on the classification; first-report timestamps can lag the actual ASX release by minutes to hours.
- Market adjustment uses the ASX 200, not a lithium benchmark, so sector-wide lithium moves remain in the numbers.
